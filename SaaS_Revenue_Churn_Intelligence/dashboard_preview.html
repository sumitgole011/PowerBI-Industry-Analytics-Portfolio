    Metric definitions

    Month-end MRR
    Recurring revenue at the end of the latest selected month. The annualized run rate multiplies it by 12.
    Logo churn
    Churned customers divided by starting customers across the selected months.
    Net revenue retention
    Starting recurring revenue adjusted for expansion, downgrades, and churn, divided by starting revenue.
